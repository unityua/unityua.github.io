# DeadBonk — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

This privacy policy explains how **DeadBonk** (the "Game"), published by **Bogus Laves Games** ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on Android.

## Summary

- The Game has **no accounts** and **no sign-in**. We do not run any servers that collect your personal data.
- Your progress is saved **only on your device**.
- The Game shows **optional rewarded video ads** provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device data as described below.

## Information stored on your device

The Game saves your settings and progress (unlocks, currency, run progress, statistics) in a file on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you use local co-op (see below). Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

## Local co-op (LAN / Wi-Fi hotspot)

When you choose to play co-op, your device connects directly to other players' devices on the **same local network**. To make this work the Game shares with those players only:

- the nickname you entered (or your device name if you did not enter one),
- a random player identifier generated on your device, used so the host can return your character if you reconnect,
- the game state needed to play together.

This information is sent only to devices on your local network during the session. We never receive it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch. AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
- information about ads shown and your interactions with them,
- diagnostic and fraud-prevention data.

This data is used to show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the same form.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (**Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

For more about how Google uses data, see: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Data security and retention

We do not store your data on our servers. Data held by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by deleting the Game's data from your device or through Google's ad settings and tools. You can always contact us for help.
