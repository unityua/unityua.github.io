# French Assault 1812 — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

This privacy policy explains how **French Assault 1812** (the "Game"), published by **Bogus Laves Games** ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on Android.

## Summary

- The Game has **no accounts** and **no sign-in**. We do not run any servers that collect your personal data.
- Your progress, settings and custom maps are saved **only on your device**.
- The current version of the Game **does not show ads**. It includes the Unity Ads software library, which can access your device's advertising ID (see below).

## Information stored on your device

The Game saves your settings (sound, music, camera speed and similar), campaign progress, nickname, chosen general's appearance and the maps you build in the level editor in files on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you play multiplayer on a local network (see below). Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

## Local multiplayer and co-op (LAN / Wi-Fi hotspot)

When you choose to play multiplayer or co-op, your device connects directly to other players' devices on the **same local network**. To make this work the Game shares with those players only:

- the nickname you entered,
- your chosen army, general's appearance, perks and room chat phrases,
- a custom map you choose to play together, if any,
- the game state needed to play together.

This information is sent only to devices on your local network during the session. We never receive it.

## Advertising ID (Unity Ads library)

The Game includes the **Unity Ads** library from Unity Technologies. The current version does not request or show ads. Because the library is included, the app declares permission to access your device's **advertising ID** and Android's ad-services APIs. If a future version of the Game shows ads, Unity Ads and its partners may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about ads shown and your interactions with them, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This policy will be updated before that happens.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (**Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

Unity's privacy policy: <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>.

## Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. The engine may send limited, non-personal technical information (such as device model, operating system version and app version) to Unity Technologies to help operate and improve the engine, as described in Unity's privacy policy above.

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Data security and retention

We do not store your data on our servers. Data held by Unity Technologies is kept according to Unity's own retention policies.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by deleting the Game's data from your device or through your device's ad settings. You can always contact us for help.

We may update this policy. The latest version is always published at this address, with the date above.
